Ziklub is a private listening room for friends who are far apart. The look is soft and cozy, like headphones on with your squad. Moods are shown by little shape-shifting characters: one is a Zu, many are Zuz.

03 · The Zuz

Eight moods, eight shapes

Each Zu has its own colour and its own way of moving. The star is en feu: it gets so hyped by the song that it shakes, then turns into a flame. Tap any Zu to send it into the room below.

The labels use casual slang in each language, and the Zuz themselves work without any words.

The player stays small

Everyone sees the song, the time and who is DJ. Only the DJ gets working controls; everyone else sees the same card with them greyed out.

Chat fills the middle

The chat takes most of the screen, and Zuz float up over it, like live reactions on Instagram or TikTok. Nothing covers the input.

Reactions are one tap

All eight Zuz sit above the keyboard, so you never open a menu to react. Try tapping them.

Change your look anytime

Tap your own Zu in the room to swap accessories. Friends see it live, with a small line in the chat.

05 · Your Zu

Dress up your Zu

No account needed. Your Zu is your photo ID in the room: pick a face, hair, beard, hat and bling, each in the colour you want. You can change your look whenever you like. Try it here.
